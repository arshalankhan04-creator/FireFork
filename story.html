The Guardian of Tradition

ARSALAN MEWATI

Executive Chef & Founder
"Tradition is not about worshipping ashes, but keeping the fire alive."

With over 20 years of experience in the royal kitchens of Rajasthan, Chef Arsalan brings the soul of India to FireFork. He is the keeper of the Tandoor. His story is simple: he believes that the soil of India holds the world's best spices, and it is his duty to unlock their potential using nothing but heat and patience.

The Architect of Flavor

RUSHAN MANSURI

Head of Culinary Operations
"Balance is not just a taste; it is a feeling."

Chef Rushan is the bridge between the old world and the new. Trained in classical French techniques but raised on Gujarati home cooking, she deconstructs traditional dishes and rebuilds them with modern elegance. Her story is one of precision—every gram of spice, every degree of heat is calculated to ensure the FireFork experience is unforgettable.

The Visionary

AHMAD SURTI

Creative Director
"We eat with our eyes first, but we remember with our hearts."

Ahmad is the storyteller. While Arsalan focuses on the pot and Rushan on the palate, Ahmad focuses on the plate. His journey began as a food photographer, capturing the steam rising from street food bowls across Mumbai. He translates the chaos and vibrancy of Indian streets into the refined, sculptural presentations you see at FireFork today.

THE HANDS BEHIND THE HEAT

Our Masters provide the vision, but the FireFork Brigade provides the muscle. These nine chefs are the unsung heroes—the prep cooks, the sauce masters, and the grill tenders. They work 12-hour shifts, tasting, adjusting, and perfecting. They are the apprentices who will one day become the masters.
